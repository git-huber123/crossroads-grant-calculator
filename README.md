# Crossroads Grant Calculator

### Description

I created this calculator because I didn't want to keep on trying to figure out
how much progress I had with my goal, and because I might as well make a project
out of it, no?

### Usage

Just input how many hours you have currently, and your pace, and the calculator
will tell you how many hours you'll have by the time the event ends (default
date is Feb 21, end of the in-person event, but you can change it)

You can also have the calculator auto-calculate your pace by inputting how many hours
you had at a previous date.

### Example

Say you have 20 hours, and 2 days ago you had 15 hours. If the date is 11/26/2026, you
input 20 for the "today" box, and then in the pace section, pick "Previous Date", input
11/24/2026 and 15 hours.

Now that it has the pace, it'll use the pace whne calculating and display how many hours you'll have
by the time the event ends.

Finally, it'll convert those hours into stamps, and then convert the stamps into grants
at the rate of 115 stamps per $25 grant.
